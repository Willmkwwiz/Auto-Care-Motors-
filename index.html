WHAT WE DO

WORKSHOP SERVICES

Honest workmanship across bodywork and painting, engine servicing, auto electrical, welding, covered car washing and towing.

01

Panel Beating & Painting

Dent straightening, panel alignment, surface preparation and clean paint refinishing, from a single panel to a full respray.

  • Dent removal & panel beating
  • Surface prep, filler leveling & priming
  • Spot, panel & full-body painting
  • Bumper, trim & rim painting
02

Engine Services

Dependable routine maintenance and mechanical diagnostics for everyday driving.

Routine Engine Care

  • Engine oil and filter changes
  • Engine checks and fault diagnosis
  • Cooling-system checks & overheating repairs
  • Spark plug replacement & tune-ups
  • Engine leak checks & minor repairs

Major Work (Confirm Availability)

  • Timing-belt replacement
  • Fuel-system repairs & engine overhauls
03

Auto Electrical

Fault finding and repair of your vehicle’s electrical system, so it starts, charges and lights up reliably.

  • Battery testing, charging & replacement
  • Alternator & starter motor checks
  • Wiring faults, fuses & relays
  • Headlamp, indicator & lighting repairs
04

Welding & Fabrication

Strong, neat weld repairs for body, underbody and metal parts, done in the workshop alongside bodywork.

  • Panel & body weld repairs
  • Rust cut-out & patch repairs
  • Chassis, underbody & bracket repairs
  • Exhaust & mounting repairs
05

Car Wash & Rollout Care

Shaded wash bays designed to keep vehicles cool during cleaning and post-repair handover.

  • Shaded canopy wash bays
  • Exterior wash & mud/dust removal
  • Wheel & tire cleaning
  • Interior vacuuming & dashboard care
  • Post-paint & service rollout wash

Towing & Long-Distance Recovery

Towing within Lilongwe and recovery from further away, for breakdowns, non-runners and accident vehicles, delivered to our workshop or another agreed destination.

  • Breakdown & non-runner recovery
  • Accident & panel-beating transport
  • Out-of-town & long-distance recovery
  • Direct intake into repair, electrical or wash bay

BEFORE YOU COME IN

COMMON QUESTIONS

Where is the workshop?

We are alongside the Bypass Road at Zankutu in Lilongwe. Use the Get Directions button in the Find Us section, or message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you in.

Do I get a price before work starts?

Yes. We give a clear estimate before any work begins. Send your vehicle details through the inquiry form or WhatsApp and we will come back to you.

Which days are you open?

Monday to Saturday. The workshop is closed on Sundays.

How do I book a service?

Open the inquiry form, choose your service, date and time, and send it to us on WhatsApp. We will confirm your slot.

Can you do major engine work?

Routine engine care is available. For timing-belt replacement, fuel-system repairs or engine overhauls, please confirm availability with us first.

Do you do auto electrical and welding work?

Yes. We handle car electrical faults and welding repairs. Tell us the symptoms or send a photo of the damage when you book so we can prepare.

Do you tow broken-down vehicles?

Yes, in Lilongwe and for longer recovery jobs away from town. Share where the vehicle is, where it needs to go, its model and condition on WhatsApp, and we will confirm the details and price before moving it.
